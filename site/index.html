International sound. Istanbul roots. Independent spirit.

More Life is an independent event production and talent booking company, founded in Istanbul in 2023. We bring international electronic music artists to Turkey, produce our own shows, and book artists for venues, festivals and brands across the country.

Music

The artist is the product. We book acts we would pay to see. International DJs and live acts, brought to Turkey with care for the artist and the audience.

Production

A party is a production. Sound, light, stage, flow and door matter as much as the lineup. From a beach-club afternoon to an open-air show, every detail has a place.

Connection

Respect the crowd. Fair pricing and honest set times. We build recurring seasons and lasting relationships with venues, agencies and the people who come for the music.

Your information.

More Life uses the contact details and messages you submit to respond to enquiries, assess partnership and table requests, and manage event updates you opt into.

Submissions are stored with the website hosting provider and are accessible only to the authorized More Life administrator. Joining the community is optional and separate from sending an enquiry.

To request deletion, correction or withdrawal from event updates, contact info@morelife.events.

Optional analytics starts only after you allow it. We measure page views, active time, scroll milestones, link clicks, table selections and successful form submissions using a random session identifier. Campaign tags and referring domains help us understand where visits come from. We do not record your screen, keystrokes, form contents or raw IP address in analytics.

Analytics records are kept in a rolling 90-day window. Your choice is stored on this device; you can decline or withdraw it at any time with Privacy choices. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. Declining does not prevent ticket access or form submission. When analytics is allowed, campaign attribution is also saved with your enquiry for follow-up. Necessary hourly hashed network identifiers are used to limit abuse.

Ticket purchases are handled by Weeztix under its own terms and privacy information. Embedded maps and media may contact their respective providers when loaded.
